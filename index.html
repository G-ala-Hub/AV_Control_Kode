What We Do

At AV Control Kode, we're dedicated to revolutionizing your audiovisual experience. We start with discussions to understand your requirements, visit the space, and engage with what the system needs.

We assist with the design, whether it's a new design or reviewing existing documentation. Detail is key. Our strength lies in meticulous programming, seamless deployment, and thorough commissioning. We don't stop there.

We conduct user training to ensure the solution meets requirements and provides a great user experience. If revisions are needed, we act immediately.Finally, we hand over your documents, code, and important system information.

From conceptualisation to execution, we ensure every aspect of your audio-visual integration enhances your space and exceeds expectations. Whether transforming a boardroom, upgrading an auditorium, or creating an immersive entertainment space, we bring your vision to life.

Our history

AV Control Kode, established in 2022, has been programming audio-visual systems since 2008. Despite being a small company, we are nimble, responsive, and capable of delivering projects on par with larger firms. We welcome collaboration and collective delivery with industry colleagues.

We are

We're AV control programming experts with over 15 years of experience. We craft seamless AV solutions that exceed the highest standards, delivering programming that is exceptional, meticulously designed, flawlessly executed, and perfectly tailored to our clients' needs.

We deliver

The short list of what we provide: System Designs, Control System Programming, Audio DSP Programming, System functionality requirements, System audits, User training and User Interface designs with great user experiences

We create and innovate

Working with technology requires continuous learning, adapting, and innovating. We implement state-of-the-art methods in every project by staying current with product options and capabilities, and through ongoing training and research. AV Control Kode is a registered Crestron Services Provider company with certifications as a Crestron Masters Programmer, Extron Authorized Programmer, AMX accredited programmer, Kramer Control certified, and QSC certification in progress.



Our Work

We specialise in enhancing and deploying systems for a variety of spaces, including Microsoft Teams and Zoom Rooms, classrooms, school halls, lecture theatres, teaching spaces, function and training rooms, boardrooms, courtrooms, streaming studios, entertainment venues like pubs and clubs, art galleries, cruise ships, and high-end residences. If you can imagine it, we can make it easier to operate or automate with our programming expertise.
